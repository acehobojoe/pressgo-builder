Hey,

Just pushed the biggest update to PressGo Builder since launch, and I wanted you to hear it from me before you see the update notice.

**Your photos now land on the first build.** This was the thing that bugged me most. You'd drop photos into chat, describe your business, and the first page would come back without them. Fixed. Tell it about your business, give it your photos, and they show up in the hero, the service cards, the gallery, right where they should be.

**Eight new layouts, and they're the ones people actually ask for:**

- A real service and menu price list. If you build pages for restaurants, salons, barbers, or trades, this is the one you've been waiting for. No more pretending a pizza menu is three SaaS pricing cards.
- A "Visit Us" section with tap-to-call phone, hours, and the map together.
- Before and after photo pairs for transformation work.
- An us-vs-them comparison section.
- A solo spotlight for when one person is the business.
- Video galleries, modern bento feature grids, and a stronger closing section with a trust checklist.

**Full dark themes.** Ask for a dark premium look and the whole page goes dark, every section, with frosted cards. No random white strips breaking the design anymore.

**Honest pages by default.** The AI will not invent testimonials, review counts, or statistics anymore. If you didn't give it real numbers, it won't make them up. Anything it can't verify gets clearly labeled so you can swap in the real thing.

Plus a long list of polish: a header bar with your phone number on every landing page, call buttons that repeat down the page the way high-converting local pages do, your brand fonts now applied everywhere (a few spots were quietly falling back to defaults), and a pile of mobile and tablet layout fixes.

Update from your WordPress dashboard (Plugins → Update) or grab it at wordpress.org/plugins/pressgo-builder. Your existing pages aren't touched, the new stuff applies to everything you build from here on.

One paragraph about your business plus your photos. That's the whole input. Try it on your next page and reply to this email with what it built you. I read every one.

Joe
